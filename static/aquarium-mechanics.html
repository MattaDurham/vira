What is physical here

The active cue changes joint angle targets through capped, equal-and-opposite internal motor torques. It never overwrites a rigid-body position. The C target reaches 2.4 radians; its achieved bend is determined by the joint dynamics. At 1.1 seconds the target has returned to neutral and the same joint motors restore a neutral target.

The independent angular motors are active controllers. Their compound forces have no proven scalar spring potential; the energy readout adds a geodesic spring estimate to actual kinetic energy for diagnosis. It does not establish passive cartilage or account for actuator work.

This is a dry, zero-gravity mechanical study, not a swimming or water-pressure model. Body masses and inertia use the uploaded skin-section approximation. Fin geometry is rendered but fin mass, fluid forces, buoyancy, body self-contact and physiological muscle coefficients are omitted. Vertical and twist cases are explicit mechanical stress cues, not claimed koi gaits.

Both cameras show the same recorded 240 Hz physics states. The actual skin, bones, face, fins and supports consume the physical transforms directly. There is no prescribed spinal pose, Gaussian re-centering, extra head swivel, FK projection or display interpolation. Fin histories observe every physical sample.
