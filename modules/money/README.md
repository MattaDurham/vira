# Money

Vira > [Modules](../README.md) > Money

What the owner actually pays for, at what cadence, and the bank connection behind it.

Subscriptions is a merchant registry, a charge ledger and a
deterministic cadence engine, fed by two evidence streams: bank charges
(Mercury) say money left; email receipts are the only source that knows the
future. Banking is the connection setup, where credentials never enter an
agent prompt or config.
