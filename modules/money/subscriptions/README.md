# Subscriptions

Vira > [Modules](../../README.md) > [Money](../README.md) > Subscriptions

The merchant registry, the charge ledger, the cadence engine, and both evidence streams.

`subscriptions.py` is the engine and window; `mercury.py` polls
charges past a watermark; `receipts.py` is the email-evidence pass;
`subs_visuals` surfaces the TC-IL morning picker inside Vira.
