# Networking

Vira > [Modules](../../README.md) > [People](../README.md) > Networking

Radar, reconnect, and circles: who deserves attention and who should meet whom.

The Networking tab of People (Radar was folded in on 2026-07-25).
Radar is two explainable engines over the CRM and the feed; Reconnect asks
which dormant ties are worth reactivating for the active search and why now;
Circles names the atlas's social clusters as evolving subjects. All three are
gather-then-ask: deterministic gathering, one sized model call.
