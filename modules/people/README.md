# People

Vira > [Modules](../README.md) > People

The CRM: who the owner knows, what the evidence says, and who to talk to next.

The People window is the memory. The CRM's records live outside Vira
(the configured CRM root) and stay the source of truth; Vira reads them in
place and writes back only hooks, open loops and new people, atomically and
backed up. On top of that read sit the contact card, the synthesized dossier,
group threads as first-class subjects, the sync spokes to Apple Contacts, and
Networking: the Radar's "who deserves attention" and "who should meet whom".
