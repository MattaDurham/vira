# CRM

Vira > [Modules](../../README.md) > [People](../README.md) > CRM

The person record, its card, its dossier, its search, and group threads.

`data.py` loads the registry, master evidence, profiles and the chat
archive index from the CRM root and answers merged person queries; it is the
only reader of those files. The contact card is the owner-authored top pane
of every person page, the profile refresh regenerates the dossier text on
demand, and the CRM index makes the whole thing a searchable database rather
than a substring scan. Vault person pages are joined in so a note about
someone is reachable from their page.
