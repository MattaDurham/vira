# Evidence

Vira > [Modules](../README.md) > Evidence

Vira's own build provenance: the ledger, the change log, and the walkthrough films.

What Vira knows about how it was built. The Evidence Ledger mines the
session record into interview-ready case studies (problem, how the owner
directed the agent, what shipped). The change log keys every shipped change
by the day it happened and owns the timeline. Walkthroughs are the animated
fly-throughs captured at the end of a build, anonymized before they are
tracked.
