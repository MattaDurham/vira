# Reader

Vira > [Modules](../README.md) > Reader

Everything worth reading, wherever it lives, and the rooms that ingest it whole.

The Reader's queue is a registry of pointers to documents living
elsewhere: audit dossiers, plan-mode output, session retros, reading-room
pages. Reading rooms are researched consumption queues - every worthwhile
talk, paper and video on a subject - and since 2026-08-05 every item's
material lands in the vault in full, never as a pointer note. The nightly
ingest is browsable as one shelf.
