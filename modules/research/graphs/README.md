# Graphs

Vira > [Modules](../../README.md) > [Research](../README.md) > Graphs

Read-only adapters over the research databases, and the claim browser.

`research.py` reads a graph's manifest and tables; nothing here
writes to the database, which is the canonical research record. The window
is `research.js`.
