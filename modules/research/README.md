# Research

Vira > [Modules](../README.md) > Research

Claim-centred evidence over local, read-only research graphs.

Research graphs are normalized public-source SQLite databases kept
with the owner's local research: canonical sources, underlying events,
deduplicated utterances, semantic claims, repost provenance. The Research
window browses them claim-first; topics make them reusable, with packets and
recurring refreshes. Find's chat can ground on a graph end to end.
