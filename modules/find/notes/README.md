# Notes

Vira > [Modules](../../README.md) > [Find](../README.md) > Notes

The vault index and grounded ask (a thin adapter over qocha), and every confined vault write.

The Obsidian vault is read through qocha, the standalone engine
extracted from here in 2026-07-20; `vault.py` is the adapter and the multi-
vault configuration. Brain chat is the persistent, vault-grounded
conversation state. `vaultwrite.py` is the one way anything writes into a
vault: destination selection and confined mutations shared by every writer,
so a writer can never change global settings or stray outside its folder.
