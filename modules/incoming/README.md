# Incoming

Vira > [Modules](../README.md) > Incoming

Every channel a message can arrive on, merged into one live feed.

The Incoming window is where the outside world reaches Vira: iMessage
read in place from chat.db, email over IMAP or Microsoft Graph, WhatsApp
through a linked-device sidecar, and the Android companion over its pairing
link. Each connector is deterministic (polls, watches, joins senders to CRM
people) and hands items to one feed with shared read/hidden state. Replies
and pushes go back out over Messages.app.

Nothing here decides what matters; that is Attention's job. Nothing here
stores a person; that is People's. This module moves messages, keeps a
durable copy of them, and sorts unknown senders into the right queue.
