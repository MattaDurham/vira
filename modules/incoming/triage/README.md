# Triage

Vira > [Modules](../../README.md) > [Incoming](../README.md) > Triage

Unknown senders, institutional chores, and correspondence that is evidence first.

Three queues for things that arrive without a home. Triage surfaces
iMessage handles no CRM person owns, with an AI resolver's guess as prefill,
and appends approved people to the CRM. The admin inbox keeps chores from
institutional senders (portals, pharmacies, carriers) out of the people
lists. Correspondence intake files private mail with explicit destinations
and durable receipts, rendering before anything runs a model.
